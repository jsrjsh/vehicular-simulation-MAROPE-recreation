\begin{abstract}
Dense vehicular edge offloading can overload single-RAT V2X systems as vehicle density increases. We propose MA-ROPE--Multi-RAT Privacy-Preserving Federated Deep Reinforcement Learning for V2X Task Offloading--which selects among LTE, ITS-G5, WiFi-6, and 5G-NR and applies SINR-proportional partial offload ratios using Dueling Double DQN with FedAvg cooperative learning. Under URLLC stress at N=32 vehicles and five seeds, MA-ROPE achieves 83.4% fleet on-time delivery versus 52.5% for the single-RAT AllOffload baseline, improves deadline-compliance utility $U_{OTR}$ by 0.178, and reduces energy by 25.2% (0.706 J vs. 0.944 J). The same setting gives 1.15% RSU queue-classified loss for MA-ROPE versus 2.14% for AllOffload. The multi-RAT controller adds no measurable simulator-step overhead relative to single-RAT offload (166.6 us vs. 167.6 us), and DQN inference remains 59.83 us per agent. Centralized MADDPG attains a slightly higher $U_{OTR}$ but assumes centralized observation sharing and uses substantially more energy. To our knowledge, MA-ROPE is the first V2X framework to combine multi-RAT selection, SINR-proportional partial offload, shared MEC queue contention, dual PRR/OTR utility reporting, and federated DRL in one simulation framework.
\end{abstract}

\section{Conclusion}

This paper presented MA-ROPE, a V2X offloading framework that combines multi-RAT heterogeneous selection, SINR-proportional partial offload, shared MEC queue contention, dual PRR/OTR utility reporting, and federated DRL. At N=32 under URLLC stress, MA-ROPE achieves 83.4\% fleet on-time delivery versus 52.5\% for single-RAT AllOffload, improves $U_{OTR}$ by 0.178, and reduces energy by 25.2\%. Safety-only analysis shows MA-ROPE preserves 0.752 safety OTR at N=32, while AllOffload falls to 0.287. MADDPG remains a centralized upper bound with slightly higher $U_{OTR}$ and lower latency, but it assumes centralized observations and consumes substantially more energy. These results position MA-ROPE as a practical privacy-preserving, multi-RAT, queue-aware controller for dense V2X offloading.

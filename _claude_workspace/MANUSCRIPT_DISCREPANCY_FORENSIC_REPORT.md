open pending more work that cannot change the outcome.

## Summary

Of 15 distinct discrepancy rows: **10 are fully resolved with a code or document citation, 2 are
resolved as ordinary statistical variance (not real discrepancies), and 3 (U_PRR × implicit,
U_OTR × implicit — really one underlying phenomenon affecting two metrics) are bounded-unresolved**
— the category of cause is established with confidence, but the specific originating configuration
cannot be recovered because it no longer exists in any accessible form. This is not a research gap
that more forensic work closes; it is a property of what did and didn't survive from whatever
produced the manuscript's original numbers.

**This does not block manuscript revision.** Every metric that will actually appear in a revised
table now has either a full explanation or an honest, bounded "we know the category, not the exact
source" disclosure — which is itself a defensible answer to a reviewer's question, more defensible
than silence or a guess. `CLAIM_BY_CLAIM_MANUSCRIPT_IMPACT.md` remains accurate as written; this
report supplies the missing citations behind it, not a reason to redo it.
